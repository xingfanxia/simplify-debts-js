Privacy policy · August 13, 2026

Your numbers stay yours.

Settle works without an account or backend. Names, expenses, and saved history remain in your browser or the app sandbox on your device.

No accountNo trackingNo data sale

01

What Settle stores

The people, expenses, preferences, and history snapshots you enter are saved locally so you can return to them later. Settle does not send this information to a Settle server because there is no Settle account system or backend.

02

What Settle does not collect

Settle does not include analytics, advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party data brokers. We do not collect, sell, rent, or use your expense data for profiling.

03

Exports and sharing

Settle creates payment text and images only when you ask it to. If you use the system share sheet, the recipient or service you choose receives that export and handles it under its own privacy terms.

04

Deletion and device backups

Delete individual history snapshots inside Settle. Clearing the site or app data, or uninstalling the app, removes its local data. Browser and iOS backups may follow your device settings; the Android app disables system backup.

05

Distribution services

Apple, GitHub, Vercel, your browser, or your network provider may process ordinary store, download, or access information independently. Their practices are outside Settle and governed by their own policies.

06

Questions

If you have a privacy question or believe something is not working as described, open an issue in the Settle repository.
